## 1. Conventions used in every definition below

- Indices $`A,B,K,L\in\lbrace0,1,\dots,7\rbrace`$ label the coordinates $`x_A`$, the octonion units $`e_A`$ and the Clifford generators; $`i,j\in\lbrace1,\dots,7\rbrace`$; $`h\in\lbrace1,2,3\rbrace`$; $`k\in\lbrace4,\dots,7\rbrace`$ where stated.
- Mathematica lists are 1-based: the entry $`\eta_{AB}`$ of a metric is `η[[A+1, B+1]]`.
- $`1_n`$ is the $`n\times n`$ identity matrix (`IdentityMatrix[n]`, named `ID4`, `ID8`, `ID16`, `ID32`); $`0`$ in a block matrix is a zero block; $`M^{\mathsf T}`$ is the transpose, $`\overline M`$ the complex conjugate, $`M^\dagger`$ the conjugate transpose; $`\delta_{AB}`$ is the Kronecker delta; $`\lbrace X,Y\rbrace=XY+YX`$, $`[X,Y]=XY-YX`$.
- $`\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{smallmatrix}\right)`$ is a block matrix (Mathematica `ArrayFlatten[{{a, b}, {c, d}}]`); $`M\otimes N`$ is the Kronecker product (`KroneckerProduct[M, N]`).
- An index 8 on a family of eight Clifford generators $`G_0,\dots,G_7`$ means the ordered product $`G_8=G_0G_1\cdots G_7`$ (the convention of 4+4.nb).
- $`Cl(p,q)`$: $`p`$ generators square to $`+1`$ and $`q`$ to $`-1`$ ($`v^2=Q(v)`$, $`Q`$ of signature $`(p,q)`$). This label is not the xAct list $`\{7,1,0\}`$, which declares seven space-like dimensions and one time-like dimension (section 2).

**The signature: $`\{7,1,0\}`$ and $`\mathrm{diag}(+1,-1,\dots,-1)`$.** The 7+1 spacetime has one time-like dimension ($`x_0`$, the direction of the octonion unit $`e_0=1`$) and seven space-like dimensions ($`x_1,x_2,x_3`$ ordinary space, $`x_4,\dots,x_7`$ auxiliary space). Two different objects describe this, and neither may be replaced by the other. (i) The tensor package xAct, used in section 12 of 7+1.nb exactly as in the author’s notebook Einstein-Lovelock-7+1-Nash.nb, declares the metric with `DefMetric[{7, 1, 0}, g71[-a, -b], CD, …]`: the list $`\{7,1,0\}`$ declares seven space-like dimensions, one time-like dimension and no degenerate dimension — the numbers of entries $`+1`$ and $`-1`$ of $`\eta^{(7,1)}=\mathrm{diag}(-1,+1,\dots,+1)`$ (`η71`), the same spacetime written in the sign convention of 4+4.nb (space-like $`=+1`$, time-like $`=-1`$). (ii) The matrix $`\eta^{(1,7)}=-\eta^{(7,1)}=\mathrm{diag}(+1,-1,\dots,-1)`$ (`η17`) is simply the squares of the octonion basis: $`e_0^2=+1`$ for the unit $`e_0=1`$ (time) and $`e_i^2=-1`$ for the seven imaginary units $`e_1,\dots,e_7`$ (space); more generally it is the real part of the product of two basis units, and it gives the quadratic form of the octonions:

```math
\begin{gathered}
\eta^{(1,7)}_{AB}=\mathrm{Re}(e_Ae_B),\qquad e_0^2=+1,\qquad e_i^2=-1\ \ (i=1,\dots,7),\\ \mathrm{Re}(x\,x)=x_0^2-x_1^2-\cdots-x_7^2=\eta^{(1,7)}_{AB}\,x^Ax^B\qquad (x=x^Ae_A\in\mathbb O).
\end{gathered}
```

$`\eta^{(1,7)}`$ gives the signs of the Clifford relation $`\Gamma^{(1,7)}_A\Gamma^{(1,7)}_B+\Gamma^{(1,7)}_B\Gamma^{(1,7)}_A=2\,\eta^{(1,7)}_{AB}\,1_{16}`$ of the real 16×16 Dirac matrices: time squares to $`+1_{16}`$ and each of the seven space directions to $`-1_{16}`$, exactly like the octonion units (in the notation $`Cl(p,q)`$, with $`p`$ generators squaring to $`+1`$ and $`q`$ to $`-1`$, the algebra $`Cl(1,7)\cong\mathbb R(16)`$). It also gives the signs of the metric components: $`g_{00}>0`$ along the time-like direction, negative squares along the space-like directions. Every component computed by 7+1.nb follows from the component matrix of the metric; the declared list fixes for xAct the sign of the determinant of the metric, `SignDetOfMetric[g71]` $`=-1`$, which is the same in both sign conventions, since $`\det \eta^{(1,7)}=\det \eta^{(7,1)}=-1`$. 7+1.nb checks `SignatureOfMetric[g71]` $`=\{7,1,0\}`$, `SignDetOfMetric[g71]` $`=-1`$, $`\mathrm{Re}(e_Ae_B)=\eta^{(1,7)}_{AB}`$ and $`\mathrm{Re}(x\,x)=\eta^{(1,7)}_{AB}x^Ax^B`$.
