## 1. Conventions used in every definition below

- Indices $`A,B,K,L\in\lbrace0,1,\dots,7\rbrace`$ label the coordinates $`x_A`$, the octonion units $`e_A`$ and the Clifford generators; $`i,j\in\lbrace1,\dots,7\rbrace`$; $`h\in\lbrace1,2,3\rbrace`$; $`k\in\lbrace4,\dots,7\rbrace`$ where stated.
- Mathematica lists are 1-based: the entry $`\eta_{AB}`$ of a metric is `η[[A+1, B+1]]`.
- $`1_n`$ is the $`n\times n`$ identity matrix (`IdentityMatrix[n]`, named `ID4`, `ID8`, `ID16`, `ID32`); $`0`$ in a block matrix is a zero block; $`M^{\mathsf T}`$ is the transpose, $`\overline M`$ the complex conjugate, $`M^\dagger`$ the conjugate transpose; $`\delta_{AB}`$ is the Kronecker delta; $`\lbrace X,Y\rbrace=XY+YX`$, $`[X,Y]=XY-YX`$.
- $`\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{smallmatrix}\right)`$ is a block matrix (Mathematica `ArrayFlatten[{{a, b}, {c, d}}]`); $`M\otimes N`$ is the Kronecker product (`KroneckerProduct[M, N]`).
- An index 8 on a family of eight Clifford generators $`G_0,\dots,G_7`$ means the ordered product $`G_8=G_0G_1\cdots G_7`$ (the convention of 4+4.nb).
- $`Cl(p,q)`$: $`p`$ generators square to $`+1`$ and $`q`$ to $`-1`$ ($`v^2=Q(v)`$, $`Q`$ of signature $`(p,q)`$).
