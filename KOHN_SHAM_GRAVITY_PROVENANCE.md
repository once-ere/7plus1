## 4. Derivations

**Reality of the bilinears.** $`\Gamma_0\Gamma^{A\dagger}\Gamma_0=\Gamma^A`$, so $`(\Gamma_0\Gamma^{A_1}\cdots\Gamma^{A_r})^\dagger=\Gamma^{A_r\dagger}\cdots\Gamma^{A_1\dagger}\Gamma_0=\Gamma_0\Gamma^{A_r}\cdots\Gamma^{A_1}=(-1)^{r(r-1)/2}\Gamma_0\Gamma^{A_1}\cdots\Gamma^{A_r}`$ (distinct indices anticommute). Multiplying by $`i^{\sigma_r}`$ with $`\sigma_r=\frac{r(r-1)}2\bmod2`$ compensates the sign: $`(i^{\sigma_r})^*(-1)^{r(r-1)/2}=i^{\sigma_r}`$.

**Fierz matrix.** Completeness, $`\sum_K(\Theta_K)_{cd}(\Theta^K)_{ab}=16\delta_{ad}\delta_{bc}`$, gives $`\sum_{K\in r}(a\Theta_Kb)(c\Theta^Kd)=\sum_JC_{rJ}(a\Theta_Jd)(c\Theta^Jb)`$ with $`C_{rJ}=\frac1{256}\sum_{K\in r}\mathrm{tr}(\Theta_K\Theta_J\Theta^K\Theta^J)`$. Moving $`\Gamma^{A}`$ through a product of $`s`$ distinct $`\Gamma`$’s gives $`(-1)^s`$ if $`A`$ is not among them and $`(-1)^{s-1}`$ if it is; for $`K`$ of rank $`r`$ sharing $`l`$ indices with $`J`$ the sign is $`(-1)^{rs-l}`$, and there are $`\binom sl\binom{8-s}{r-l}`$ such $`K`$:

```math
F_{rs}=\frac{16}{256}\sum_l(-1)^{rs-l}\binom{s}{l}\binom{8-s}{r-l}=\frac1{16}\sum_l(-1)^{rs-l}\binom{s}{l}\binom{8-s}{r-l}.
```

**Densities of uniform matter.** A 16-component field has 8 positive-energy states per momentum, so $`\rho=8\int\frac{d^7k}{(2\pi)^7}`$ over the Fermi ball (comoving, divided by the comoving volume factor $`a^3b^4`$). With $`k_a=k\cos\theta`$, $`k_b=k\sin\theta`$:

```math
\begin{gathered}
d^3k_a\,d^4k_b=(4\pi)(2\pi^2)\,k^6\cos^2\theta\sin^3\theta\,dk\,d\theta,\qquad \frac{8\cdot8\pi^3}{(2\pi)^7}=\frac1{2\pi^4},\\ \int_0^{\pi/2}\cos^2\theta\sin^3\theta\,d\theta=\frac2{15},\qquad \rho=\frac{k_F^7}{105\pi^4a^3b^4}.
\end{gathered}
```

The kinetic pressures are the momentum flux per direction: three ordinary directions share $`k_a^2/a^2`$, four auxiliary directions share $`k_b^2/b^2`$, which gives $`p_a=\langle k^2\cos^2\theta/(3a^2\omega)\rangle+\Pi`$ and $`p_b=\langle k^2\sin^2\theta/(4b^2\omega)\rangle+\Pi`$; the interaction pressure is $`\Pi=\rho\,\partial\varepsilon_{\rm int}/\partial\rho-\varepsilon_{\rm int}`$ of the mean-field energy $`\varepsilon_{\rm int}=-\frac{(M-m)^2}{2G_S}+\frac{G_V}2\rho^2`$ at fixed $`M`$.

**Conservation.** $`\varepsilon(a,b,M)`$ is stationary in $`M`$ (that is the gap equation), so $`\varepsilon'=\partial_a\varepsilon\,a'+\partial_b\varepsilon\,b'`$; differentiating the comoving integrals ($`\rho\propto a^{-3}b^{-4}`$, $`\partial_a\omega=-k^2\cos^2\theta/(a^3\omega)`$) gives

```math
\varepsilon'=-3H_a(\varepsilon+p_a)-4H_b(\varepsilon+p_b).
```

**Constraint preservation.** The Lovelock tensors are divergence-free for every metric, $`\nabla_\mu E^{(k)\mu}{}_\nu=0`$; for the cosmological metric the $`\nu=0`$ component reads $`\frac{d}{dx_0}E^{(k)0}{}_0+3H_a(E^{(k)0}{}_0-E^{(k)1}{}_1)+4H_b(E^{(k)0}{}_0-E^{(k)4}{}_4)=0`$. With the 11 and 44 equations imposed and the conservation law above, the 00 equation therefore holds at all times if it holds initially.
